About Tree Soul Genesis (TSG)

Tree Soul Genesis (TSG) is a Monad Network (ERC-20) based green ecosystem project that aims to create long-term value through stability, transparency, and community participation.

With a total supply of 93,000,000 TSG, the project adopts a gradual, strong, and sustainable tree-growing philosophy to reflect the growth journey of Web3 projects.

TSG connects nature and blockchain technology, delivering a healthy, stable, and community-friendly ecosystem through utilities, NFTs, reward systems, and future Web3 features.

Manifesto

Building slowly. Growing responsibly. Lasting longer.

Tree Soul Genesis (TSG) was born from a simple belief: real ecosystems are not rushed — they are grown.

In a space often driven by short-term speculation, Tree Soul Genesis chooses a different path. We believe Web3 should be balanced, transparent, and community-driven, not fueled by hype or empty promises.

Nature does not grow overnight. Strong roots come first.

TSG is inspired by the philosophy of a tree: Roots represent foundations and values, Trunk represents stability and resilience, Branches represent utility, community, and expansion, Leaves represent users and contributors.

Is the liquidity (LP) safe?

Liquidity is managed with a long-term stability mindset. Important note: Liquidity is not withdrawn arbitrarily and is handled transparently. Liquidity locking options will be considered as the project matures.

Why does the largest wallet look so big?

The largest wallet shown on explorers is the liquidity wallet, not a personal developer wallet. This is a common structure in blockchain projects to maintain market depth and trading stability.

Is the developer anonymous?

No. The developer is active and involved in managing the project. Identity is presented responsibly to balance transparency and security during the early development phase.

Why is the website hosted on GitHub Pages?

The website is currently hosted on GitHub Pages as an early-stage solution that is free, stable, transparent, and open-source friendly. A custom domain will be introduced in a future phase.

Will there be NFTs?

Yes. TSG will introduce Genesis NFTs as early community identity, a symbol of early participation, and access to future utilities.

Is TSG audited?

A smart contract audit will be considered once the project reaches a more mature phase and requires additional external validation.
