About TSG

Tentang TSG

Tree Soul Genesis (TSG) is a Monad Network (ERC-20) based green ecosystem project that aims to create long-term value through stability, transparency, and community participation. With a total supply of 93,000,000 TSG, the project adopts a gradual, strong, and sustainable tree-growing philosophy to reflect the growth journey of Web3 projects.

TSG connects nature and blockchain technology, delivering a healthy, stable, and community-friendly ecosystem through utilities, NFTs, reward systems, and future Web3 features.

Tree Soul Genesis (TSG) adalah proyek ekosistem hijau berbasis Monad Network (ERC-20) yang bertujuan menciptakan nilai jangka panjang melalui stabilitas, transparansi, dan partisipasi komunitas. Dengan total supply 93.000.000 TSG, proyek ini mengadopsi filosofi pertumbuhan pohon yang bertahap, kuat, dan berkelanjutan untuk mencerminkan perjalanan proyek Web3.

TSG menghubungkan alam dan teknologi blockchain, memberikan ekosistem yang sehat, stabil, dan ramah komunitas melalui utilitas, NFT, sistem reward, dan fitur Web3 masa depan.

🌱 TREE SOUL GENESIS (TSG) — COMMUNITY FAQ

**Summary:** ❓ What is Tree Soul Genesis (TSG)?

Tree Soul Genesis (TSG) is a Web3 green ecosystem project built on the Monad Network, focused on stability, transparency, and long-term community growth.
TSG follows a “grow slow, grow strong” philosophy — just like a tree.

**Summary:** ❓ Is TSG a long-term project?

Yes. TSG is designed with a gradual and realistic approach, without instant promises. Our long-term focus includes ecosystem stability, active and healthy community, and sustainable development.

**Summary:** ❓ Does TSG promise profits?

No. TSG is not financial advice and does not guarantee profits. Token value depends on adoption, community activity, and ecosystem development. Always do your own research (DYOR).

**Summary:** ❓ Is the liquidity (LP) safe?

Liquidity is managed with a long-term stability mindset. Currently, liquidity is not locked via a smart contract, however: Liquidity is not withdrawn arbitrarily, it is not used for personal purposes, and all actions are handled transparently. Liquidity locking options will be considered as the project matures.

**Summary:** ❓ Why does the largest wallet look so big?

The largest wallet shown on explorers is the liquidity wallet, not a personal developer wallet. This is a common structure in blockchain projects to maintain market depth and trading stability.

**Summary:** ❓ Is the developer anonymous?

No. The developer is active and involved in managing the project. Identity is presented responsibly to balance transparency and security during the early development phase.

**Summary:** ❓ Why is the website hosted on GitHub Pages?

The website is currently hosted on GitHub Pages as an early-stage solution that is free, stable, transparent and open-source friendly. A custom domain will be introduced in a future phase as the project grows.

**Summary:** ❓ Will there be NFTs?

Yes. TSG will introduce Genesis NFTs as early community identity, a symbol of early participation, and access to future utilities. Details will be announced gradually.

**Summary:** ❓ Is TSG audited?

A smart contract audit will be considered once the project reaches a more mature phase and requires additional external validation.
